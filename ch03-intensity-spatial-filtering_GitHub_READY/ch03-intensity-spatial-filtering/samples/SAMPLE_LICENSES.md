# Sample image notes

Bundled third-party sample images come from `skimage.data` and were selected because their current scikit-image documentation states permissive/no-known-restriction status:

- `camera_cc0.png`: CC0 by photographer Lav Varshney.
- `text_public_domain.png`: released into the public domain.
- `clock_public_domain.png`: released into the public domain by photographer Stefan van der Walt.
- `coins_no_known_restrictions.png`: Brooklyn Museum source; scikit-image documents no known copyright restrictions.

The remaining `*_synthetic.png` images are generated numerically by this project.

The GUI also exposes additional `skimage.data` images at runtime. Those images remain provided by the installed scikit-image package rather than being redistributed here.
